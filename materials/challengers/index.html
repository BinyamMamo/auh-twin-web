Challenger round: can anything beat the CatBoost hybrid?

Pre-registered (committed before any challenger result was computed)

Incumbent. The CatBoost hybrid: anchored chain plus a CatBoost residual model, using the existing hyperparameters.

Test beds. - Primary: Singapore real data. The same 8 rolling origins (2015-01 to 2018-07), 12-month horizon, market x month WMAPE. - Check: Abu Dhabi synthetic data. The 3 normal-window cutoffs. A challenger must not be worse here by more than 0.2 points.

Challengers. Defined now; no tuning after seeing results. 1. LightGBM hybrid. The same anchored target and features as CatBoost, swapping only the learner (default-style settings: 500 trees, learning rate 0.03, 15 leaves). This tests whether the gain comes from the hybrid design or from CatBoost itself. 2. Ensemble, mean. The equal-weight average of CatBoost hybrid, random-forest hybrid and Prophet. These are the incumbent plus the two strongest different model families on Singapore. Combinations are among the most reliable gains in forecasting competitions. 3. Ensemble, median. The median of the same three models. 4. Chronos-2 (Amazon, 2025). A pretrained time-series foundation model, used zero-shot with arriving passengers as a known-future covariate. It is run only if it installs and runs within about 45 minutes; otherwise it is reported as "not run".

Adoption rule. - A challenger replaces the incumbent only if it beats it on Singapore and the gain holds up under a paired block bootstrap. The bootstrap resamples whole test months 2,000 times; the 95% interval of the WMAPE difference must lie entirely below 0. - It must also not be more than 0.2 points worse on the Abu Dhabi synthetic check. - Otherwise the incumbent stays, even if a challenger is nominally lower.

Round 2 (pre-registered after round 1, before computing any round-2 number)

Motivation. In round 1, Chronos-2 zero-shot scored 6.06% on Singapore, beating the incumbent, and was best on the total. It failed the Abu Dhabi check (9.50%). Both research tracks (the web agent's check of fev-bench, and NotebookLM) recommend using foundation models as a diverse ensemble member rather than alone.

New incumbent. The round-1 winner: the median of the CatBoost hybrid, random-forest hybrid and Prophet.

Candidates. No other changes, no weights, no tuning: - R2-A: median of {CatBoost hybrid, RF hybrid, Prophet, Chronos-2} - R2-B: mean of {CatBoost hybrid, RF hybrid, Prophet, Chronos-2} - R2-C: median of {CatBoost hybrid, RF hybrid, Chronos-2} - R2-D: mean of {CatBoost hybrid, RF hybrid, Chronos-2}

Adoption rule. The same as round 1, but corrected for testing 4 candidates (Bonferroni): - the 98.75% bootstrap interval of the Singapore WMAPE difference must lie entirely below 0; - the candidate must not be more than 0.2 points worse than the incumbent on Abu Dhabi.

If several candidates qualify, the one with the lowest Singapore WMAPE is adopted.

Round 3 (pre-registered after round 2, before computing any round-3 number)

Motivation. The web research (fev-bench, checked by the agent) and NotebookLM both rank TabPFN-TS and TimesFM 2.5 next to Chronos-2 on covariate tasks. The other AI's suggestion of TabPFN is covered here.

New incumbent. R2-A, the median of {CatBoost hybrid, RF hybrid, Prophet, Chronos-2}.

New members. Zero-shot, default settings, no tuning: - TimesFM 2.5 (200M, Apache-2.0) with covariates. Seats are passed as a dynamic numerical covariate, xreg_mode="xreg + timesfm", ridge 0. Context is all history up to the cutoff. - TabPFN-TS with the original TabPFN v2 weights. Run locally. Default TabPFN-TS features, with seats as a known-future covariate. The newer v3.5 weights need a licence and API key, so they are not used in this round.

Candidates: - R3-A: median of {incumbent members + TimesFM 2.5} (5 models) - R3-B: median of {incumbent members + TabPFN-TS} (5 models) - R3-C: median of {incumbent members + both} (6 models)

Standalone scores for TimesFM 2.5 and TabPFN-TS are reported for information only.

Adoption rule. - Bonferroni for 3 candidates: the 98.33% paired block bootstrap interval of the Singapore difference against the incumbent must lie entirely below 0. - On Abu Dhabi, no more than 0.2 points worse than the incumbent. - If several qualify, the lowest Singapore WMAPE wins.
